I recently learned that IBM patented continued fractions (the details of which I'm unaware of) which got me thinking about how absurd our patent system is. This kind of systemic dysfunction reflects a deeper issue: we lack national priorities. That in turn got me to think about something else: America needs a todo list. We have no real direction as a country, no real idea as to what should be done to iteratively improve this country and the lives of those in it. Instead, things are reactionary. Or at best, we have lofty goals with no clear path or actionable steps towards them, like universal healthcare and education. Without concrete, actionable steps, politicians can campaign on lofty ideas while facing zero accountability for ever making them real.

Perhaps an interesting measure of not only a civilization, but a corporation, and even an individual, is their capacity to disambiguate ideas and goals into actionable steps.
