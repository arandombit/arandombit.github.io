A civilization is one where groups of people form explicit and implicit agreements on the rules of governance and are able to problem solve cooperatively against the challenges of life. The main problems facing any given civilization are those of resource acquisition (e.g., food, building materials, and later on, fuel), information sharing, construction, cohesiveness, and ultimately competition against other civilizations (through war or otherwise). Culture is what is rewarded and punished, and it is something that must be built around these problems.

In a nutshell, it would seem that a culture where a civilization progresses would be one where there exists simulacra of complexity around certain problems. Games and activities around the construction of things, like legos, would some such examples.
