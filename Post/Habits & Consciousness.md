The weight of someone's cognition is perhaps their capacity to understand the depth of not only the awareness in others but also the vast, cosmic depths of the habits that permeate life, people, and in turn, civilizations. This has interesting implications for how well people are at forecasting the future. It's easy to not take into account the cognitive habits that are tied to problem solving, which are bound by the tech debt of civilizational habits, which in turn has implications for how technologies manifest at different levels of society and how they propagate across these levels.
