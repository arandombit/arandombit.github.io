I've come to enjoy reading translated Japanese literature ever since reading _Days at the Morisaki Bookshop_. In it were listed many classics and contemporaries of Japanese literature, with many still untranslated. Many of these were mentioned offhand in scenarios like the store regular first meeting Takako and mentioning how he wanted to read Naoya Shiga again since his wife had thrown out his previous copies of him. I've compiled a list below of the ones mentioned so that I don't forget:

Authors:
- Naoya Shiga (志賀直哉)
- Santōka Taneda (種田山頭火)
- Murō Saisei (室生犀星)
- Kafū Nagai (永井荷風)
- Jun'ichirō Tanizaki (谷崎潤一郎)
- Shūji Tsushima (津島修治) (known by his pen name Osamu Dazai (太宰治))
- Haruo Satō (佐藤春夫)
- Ryūnosuke Akutagawa (芥川 龍之介)
- Kōji Uno (宇野 浩二)
- Motojirō Kajii (梶井基次郎)
- Naoe Kinoshita (木下尚江)

Manga Artists:
- Osamu Tezuka (手塚治虫)
- Shotaro Ishinomori (石ノ森章太郎)

Books:
- Until the Death of the Girl (或る少女の死まで)
- The Confession of a Husband (良人の自白)
