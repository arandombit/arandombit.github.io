The development of cognition follows a certain trajectory of increasing awareness which is reflected at a civilizational scale. As cognition develops, previous forms of sense and meaning making lose their luster and ultimately break down altogether. Through these cycles a civilization can have a meaning crisis until a new narrative is spun in such a way as to bind this new awareness of the world and how it works and ultimately where it's all headed.

Nietzsche conceptualized the Übermensch as an individual that would create a new system of values (morality) that would transcend the systems before it. But perhaps this is more cyclical than monolithic as he initially thought it to be.

> "As you walk on the way, the way appears" — Rumi

This has interesting implications in that as technology devolves the minds that use it, newer and simpler narratives take hold that allow those minds to make sense of the world.
