There's been a consistent decline in the trust of institutions. The same erosion of trust in institutions happened around the printing press revolution. People are no longer trusting the Harvards of the world. If we squint our eyes a bit, what seems likely to emerge is that people will now flock to certain individuals to make sense of the world for them. These 'in-betweens' (Martin Luther being one back in the day) will perhaps lead the way to new forms of institutions we'll have that people will ultimately settle on.
