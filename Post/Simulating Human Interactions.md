As agents improve and begin to simulate human behavior, it stands to reason that at some point in the future, agents will be sophisticated enough to simulate a human under various contexts, in combination with other humans to see how they interact, what their compatabilities are and how their interactions would play out over time. But at the same time, such implementations would necessarily rely on an understanding of the human animal, which at the moment is still rather primitive
